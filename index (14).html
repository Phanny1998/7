Cortado Works: great coffee, late deliveries

You join a change team at Cortado Works, a European maker of coffee machines. Customers are unhappy, margins are slipping, and every department has its own explanation.

Your job: listen to the people involved, find out what really goes wrong, and see how a joint planning process could fix it.

The case

The company and the trigger

The company

Cortado Works has built coffee machines for more than a hundred years. It started with coffee grinders, moved into professional machines for hotels, restaurants and canteens, and today also sells espresso and capsule machines through large retailers across Europe.

    The trigger

    Over the last five years Cortado Works grew fast. This year management launched an ambitious inventory reduction program, and it worked: stock is down significantly. But since then, complaints have piled up.

    From: Lead Buyer, a large department store chain · To: Head of Sales, Cortado Works

    We look back on a long and fruitful relationship. However, we are not satisfied with your current delivery reliability. Availability on our shelves is poor and below what we agreed last year. Under these circumstances, we will reduce the total invoiced amount by 2%, as provided for in our contract.

    Two percent of the invoice is real money, and it is unlikely to be the only customer thinking this way.
